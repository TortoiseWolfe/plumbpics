PlumbPics

Walk around to floor plan.

Built for remodel contractors and carpenters who burn hours driving back to re-measure houses. Walk around a building with your phone. You get a measured 3-D model and a floor plan. One visit, every dimension.

How it works

1. The walk. The app tells you where to stand and which way to point. The camera only opens when the phone is plumb, true vertical, facing the right way, so every photo knows exactly where it stood. Pro iPhones add LiDAR depth to every shot.
2. The build. The walk comes back to the office, where it is lined up against the building's model and checked photo by photo. The app captures the building in the field. The model is built on the back end, by hand, from those photos.
3. What you get. A model and plans you can measure off, quote off, and plan the job from, without a second trip.

The deliverables

  • A measured 3-D model of the building as it stands
  • A floor plan (PDF and DXF)
  • As-built documentation for the job file

From a real walk

What comes out of a PlumbPics walk. These are the finished models and plans, built from the walk photos and drawn in SketchUp. They are the pipeline's output, not screenshots from the phone app.

Who it is for

Remodelers, general contractors, carpenters, anyone whose job starts from a building that is already there. If getting the existing house down right is the slowest part of your jobs, this is built for you.
